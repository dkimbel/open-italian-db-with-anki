Nuovo Vocabolario di Base della Lingua Italiana (NVdB)
=====================================================

Source: https://www.internazionale.it/opinione/tullio-de-mauro/2016/12/23/il-nuovo-vocabolario-di-base-della-lingua-italiana

License Status
--------------
The NVdB was published online by Internazionale without an explicit open license.
No Creative Commons or other standard license was applied to the data. Standard
copyright applies under Italian law (Legge 22 aprile 1941, n. 633) and the EU
Database Directive (96/9/EC).

The word list may be protected both as a creative database (the scholarly selection
and arrangement of ~7,500 words from the full Italian lexicon) and under sui generis
database rights (the substantial research investment in corpus analysis and
psycholinguistic testing).

Copyright holders are believed to be the authors (Tullio De Mauro, deceased 2017;
Isabella Chiari) and/or Internazionale S.p.A.

Additional Notes
----------------
- Contains ~7,500 Italian words classified into usage tiers:
  FO (fondamentale, ~2,000 words covering ~90% of text),
  AU (alto uso, ~2,750 words), and AD (alta disponibilità, ~2,300 words)
- PDF extracted and structured by: https://github.com/pettarin/nvdb (CC BY 4.0
  for documentation, public domain for processed files, AGPL-3.0 for code)
- Also available as a dataset at: https://github.com/memdevice/nvdb (no license)
- All use should be for research and/or personal purposes only, consistent with
  the terms under which the data was originally published
