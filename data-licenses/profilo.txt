Profilo della lingua italiana (CEFR Word Lists)
================================================

Source: Profilo della lingua italiana - livelli di riferimento del QCER
        A1, A2, B1, B2
URL: https://www.unistrapg.it/profilo_lingua_italiana/
Authors: Barbara Spinelli and Francesca Parizzi
Publisher: La Nuova Italia / RCS Libri (now Mondadori Education), 2010
ISBN: 978-88-221-6579-1

The word lists ("liste lessicali") are hosted publicly on the website of the
University for Foreigners of Perugia (CVCL - Centro per la Valutazione e le
Certificazioni Linguistiche).

License Status
--------------
No explicit open license (CC, MIT, etc.) was found on the website or in the
published book. The data consists of factual word-to-CEFR-level assignments.

Usage in This Project
---------------------
Used as metadata enrichment for non-commercial educational purposes.
CEFR level assignments are stored with full source attribution
(source='profilo') and original word/POS provenance.
