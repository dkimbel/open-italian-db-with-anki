Additional Notes
----------------
- Contains ~2,090 Italian sentences with parallel English translations
- All sentences have full morphological annotation (lemma, POS, features)
- Sources include JRC Acquis, Creative Commons texts, and Wikipedia
- See https://universaldependencies.org/treebanks/it_partut/index.html for details
