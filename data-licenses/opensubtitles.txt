OpenSubtitles v2024 Parallel Sentences (OPUS)
==============================================

Source: OPUS OpenSubtitles v2024 parallel corpus
URL: https://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles/v2024/en-it
Corpus: Italian-English parallel sentences from movie/TV subtitles

License Status
--------------
The licensing of OpenSubtitles data distributed through OPUS is complex and
layered, with different entities asserting different terms:

1. OPUS project: Does not claim ownership of the text. States "We do not own
   any of the text from which the data has been extracted." Requests attribution
   to opensubtitles.org. No formal license specified on the download page.

2. META-SHARE (EU language resource repository): Catalogs the OPUS Subtitles
   Corpus under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-
   ShareAlike 3.0 Unported).
   https://metashare.csc.fi/repository/browse/opus-subtitles-corpus/

3. OpenSubtitles.org Terms of Service: Explicitly restricts use to
   "non-commercial, scientific and educational purposes." Commercial use is
   prohibited under their terms.

4. Hugging Face (Helsinki-NLP/open_subtitles): Lists the license as "unknown."

Taken together, the most defensible interpretation is that OpenSubtitles data
carries noncommercial restrictions. This project treats the data accordingly.

Additional Notes
----------------
- Contains ~5M Italian-English parallel sentence pairs (sampled from full corpus)
- Used in this project for example sentences and Stanza-derived frequency data
- Preprocessed during download: HTML cleaned, length-filtered, deduplicated
